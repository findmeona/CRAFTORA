Our story

We got tired of not knowing who made anything

Craftora started in 2021 as a shared spreadsheet between three friends who kept buying gifts from craft fairs and wishing there was one place to find them again. Today it's a marketplace for over 60 independent workshops — each one visited, verified, and given a page of its own.

What we won't compromise on

Three rules every maker agrees to

Name every hand

Every product page credits the specific maker or small team who made it — not a brand umbrella.

Disclose materials fully

Species of wood, clay body, leather tannage, fiber content — listed plainly, no marketing euphemisms.

Fair maker pay

Makers set their own prices. Craftora takes a flat marketplace fee, disclosed in every maker agreement.

The workshops

A few of the makers behind the catalog
